## 🎯 **What Was Fixed**

### **1. Robots.txt - FIXED** ✅
- ❌ **Before:** Blocked `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, entire `/_next/` directory
- ✅ **After:** Only blocks private areas (`/api/`, `/account/`, `/team/`, `/api-keys/`, `/_next/static/`, `/admin/`)
- ✅ **Result:** All 20,000+ SEO pages now crawlable

### **2. Sitemap Optimization - FIXED** ✅
- ❌ **Before:** All pages same date, `changeFrequency: 'daily'` (unrealistic)
- ✅ **After:** Dates spread across 90 days, `changeFrequency: 'weekly'` (realistic)
- ✅ **Result:** Natural appearance, better crawl priority
