## 1. Google Business Profile Setup

**Time Required**: 20 minutes  
**Importance**: HIGH - Helps with local SEO and brand visibility

### Step 1: Access Google Business Profile
1. Open your browser
2. Go to: https://business.google.com
3. Click the blue **"Manage now"** button
4. Sign in with your Google account (use humanifylab1@gmail.com if possible)

### Step 2: Add Your Business
1. Click **"Add business"** or **"Create a profile"**
2. Enter business name: **HumanifyLab**
3. Click **"Next"**

### Step 3: Choose Business Category
1. In the search box, type: **Software Company**
2. Select **"Software Company"** from the dropdown
3. Alternative categories you can add:
   - Technology Company
   - Internet Company
   - Computer Software Company
4. Click **"Next"**

### Step 4: Add Location (Optional)
1. If you have a physical office:
   - Select **"Yes, I have a location customers can visit"**
   - Enter your address
2. If you're online-only (recommended for SaaS):
   - Select **"No, I don't have a location customers can visit"**
   - This is fine for software businesses
3. Click **"Next"**

### Step 5: Service Area (Online Business)
1. Select: **"Yes, I serve customers outside my location"**
2. Enter service areas:
   - United States
   - Canada
   - United Kingdom
   - Australia
   - Or select: **"I serve customers everywhere"**
3. Click **"Next"**

### Step 6: Add Contact Information
1. **Phone number**: Add your business phone (optional)
2. **Website**: https://www.humanifylab.com
3. Click **"Next"**

### Step 7: Verification
1. Google will ask to verify your business
2. Choose verification method:

## 5. Google Search Console Submission

**Time Required**: 30 minutes  
**Importance**: CRITICAL - Get your pages indexed

### Step 1: Access Google Search Console
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Select your property: **www.humanifylab.com**

### Step 2: Submit Individual URLs (Priority Pages)

**For each URL below, follow these steps**:

1. Click **"URL Inspection"** in the left sidebar
2. Paste the URL in the search box
3. Press Enter
4. Wait for inspection to complete
5. Click **"Request Indexing"**
6. Wait for confirmation (may take 1-2 minutes)

**Priority URLs to Submit** (do these first):

```
https://www.humanifylab.com/
https://www.humanifylab.com/pricing
https://www.humanifylab.com/faq
https://www.humanifylab.com/contact
https://www.humanifylab.com/ai-humanizer
https://www.humanifylab.com/humanize-ai-text
https://www.humanifylab.com/bypass-turnitin
https://www.humanifylab.com/free-ai-humanizer
https://www.humanifylab.com/bypass-gptzero
https://www.humanifylab.com/humanizer
```

### Step 3: Submit Sitemap
1. Click **"Sitemaps"** in the left sidebar
2. Enter sitemap URL: **sitemap.xml**
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Also submit: **sitemaps/sitemap-index.xml**

### Step 4: Check Coverage
1. Click **"Coverage"** in the left sidebar
2. Review any errors
3. Fix errors if found
4. Re-submit fixed URLs

### Step 5: Monitor Performance
1. Click **"Performance"** in the left sidebar
2. Check impressions and clicks
3. Monitor keyword rankings
4. Identify opportunities

### Step 6: Set Up Email Alerts
1. Click the gear icon (Settings)
2. Go to **"Users and permissions"**
3. Ensure your email is added
4. Enable email notifications for:
   - Critical issues
   - New issues
   - Manual actions

**✅ Done! Your pages are submitted for indexing!**

**Note**: Indexing can take 1-7 days. Check back regularly.
